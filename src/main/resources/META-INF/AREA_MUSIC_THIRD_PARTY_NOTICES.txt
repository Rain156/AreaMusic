AreaMusic embeds the following Java Sound decoder libraries directly in its distributable mod JAR:

- MP3SPI 1.9.5.4, JLayer 1.0.1.4, and Tritonus Share 0.3.7.4
  https://github.com/pdudits/soundlibs
- VorbisSPI 1.0.3.3
  https://github.com/pdudits/soundlibs
- jFLAC Codec 1.5.2
  https://github.com/nguillaumin/jflac

Minecraft already supplies JOrbis 0.0.17. AreaMusic intentionally does not embed
the soundlibs JOrbis repack, because both JARs resolve to the automatic module
name "jorbis" and cannot coexist in the loader module layer.
