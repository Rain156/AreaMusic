AreaMusic embeds the following Java Sound decoder libraries through Forge Jar-in-Jar:

- MP3SPI 1.9.5.4 and JLayer 1.0.1.4
  https://github.com/pdudits/soundlibs
- VorbisSPI 1.0.3.3 and JOrbis 0.0.17.4
  https://github.com/pdudits/soundlibs
- jFLAC Codec 1.5.2
  https://github.com/nguillaumin/jflac
